Quick Recap
Definition

What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications, and personal information from cyber attacks and unauthorized access.

It includes technologies, processes, and security practices that help keep digital systems safe.

Protects data
Prevents attacks
Keeps systems secure
Importance

Why Cybersecurity Matters

Online Banking

Protects financial information.

Social Media

Keeps personal accounts secure.

Businesses

Protects company data and systems.

Core Concept

The CIA Triad

Confidentiality

Information should only be accessible to authorized people.

Integrity

Data should remain accurate and unchanged.

Availability

Systems and data should be available when needed.

Common Threats

Types of Cyber Threats

Cyber attacks come in many forms. Understanding them is the first step toward protecting yourself online.

Phishing

Fake emails or messages that trick users into revealing sensitive information.

Malware

Harmful software designed to damage devices or steal data.

Password Attacks

Attackers try to guess or steal passwords to access accounts.

Ransomware

Malware that locks files and demands payment to restore access.

Network Attacks

Attacks targeting Wi-Fi networks, servers, or internet traffic.

Identity Theft

Criminals steal personal information to impersonate victims.

Best Practices

Stay Safe Online

✔ Use strong passwords
✔ Enable Multi-Factor Authentication
✔ Keep software updated
✔ Never click suspicious links
✔ Install antivirus software
✔ Backup important files regularly
Did You Know?

Interesting Cybersecurity Facts

Cybersecurity affects everyone. Here are a few facts that show why staying informed is so important.

01

Cyber Attacks Never Sleep

Millions of cyber attacks are attempted every day across the world, targeting individuals, businesses, and governments.

02

Weak Passwords Are Risky

Using simple passwords makes it much easier for attackers to gain access to your online accounts.

03

People Are Often Targeted

Many attacks rely on tricking people into clicking links or sharing information rather than breaking into systems directly.

04

Everyone Needs Cybersecurity

Students, professionals, businesses, and families all benefit from good cybersecurity habits.

Common Mistakes

Avoid These Cybersecurity Mistakes

Many cyber attacks succeed because of simple mistakes. Avoid these habits to improve your online security.

Weak Passwords

Using short or predictable passwords makes it easier for attackers to access your accounts.

Clicking Suspicious Links

Never click links in unexpected emails or messages without verifying the sender.

Ignoring Updates

Software updates often include security fixes that protect against newly discovered vulnerabilities.

Unsafe Public Wi-Fi

Avoid accessing sensitive information over unsecured public Wi-Fi networks.

Downloading Unknown Files

Install software only from trusted sources to reduce the risk of malware infections.

Sharing OTPs & Passwords

Never share passwords or one-time passwords (OTPs), even if someone claims to be from a trusted organization.

Real-Life Examples

Cybersecurity in Everyday Life

Cybersecurity isn't just for IT professionals. It protects the apps, services, and devices you use every day.

Online Banking

Banks use encryption and multi-factor authentication to keep your money and personal information secure.

Social Media

Strong passwords and privacy settings help protect your social media accounts from hackers.

Online Shopping

Secure payment gateways protect your card details while you shop online.

Education

Schools and universities protect student records and online learning platforms.

Businesses

Companies secure customer data, emails, and financial information from cyber threats.

Healthcare

Hospitals use cybersecurity to protect patient records and critical medical systems.

Key Terms

Learn the Essential Terms

These are some of the most common cybersecurity terms you'll encounter. Understanding them will make future lessons much easier.

🦠 Malware

Malicious software designed to damage, steal, or disrupt computer systems.

🔥 Firewall

A security system that monitors and filters incoming and outgoing network traffic.

🔒 Encryption

The process of converting readable data into unreadable code to protect it.

🌐 VPN

A Virtual Private Network creates a secure and private internet connection.

🎣 Phishing

A cyber attack that tricks users into revealing sensitive information.

🛡 Authentication

The process of verifying a user's identity before granting access.

👨‍💻 Hacker

A person who accesses computer systems. Hackers can be ethical or malicious.

💾 Backup

A copy of important data that can be restored if the original is lost or damaged.
