How the Internet Works

Every website you visit follows the same journey. Your request travels through different devices before reaching a web server and returning the webpage to your browser in just a few milliseconds.

Your Device

Your Device

You type a website address or click a link. Your browser prepares a request to send over the Internet.

Router

Wi-Fi Router

The router forwards your request from your device to your Internet Service Provider.

ISP

Internet Service Provider

Connects your device to the global internet.

Internet

Internet

Carries your request across thousand of networks.

Server

DNS Sever

Converts the website name(google.com) into an IP address.

Website

WebSite

Your browser receives the data and displays the webpage.
