Frequently Asked Questions

Have Questions?

Here are answers to some common questions about learning cybersecurity with CyberShield.

What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, and personal data from cyber attacks and unauthorized access.

Do I need programming knowledge?

No. Our beginner lessons focus on concepts first. Programming is optional for advanced cybersecurity topics.

Is CyberShield free?

Yes. CyberShield is an educational project created to help beginners learn cybersecurity concepts for free.

Where should I start?

Start with Cybersecurity Basics and follow the learning roadmap to build your knowledge step by step.

How long does each lesson take?

Most lessons can be completed in 5–10 minutes, making it easy to learn at your own pace.
